Use Case: Bill Payment (Event-Driven Architecture)
1. Use Case Name

Event-Driven Bill Payment Processing

2. Primary Actor

Customer (via digital channel – mobile/web)

3. Supporting Actors

Payment Orchestrator Service

Biller Service

Account Service

Outbox Publisher

BillPay Worker

Settlement Service

Dummy Central 1 (External system)

Kafka Event Bus

4. Goal

To enable a customer to pay a utility bill, ensuring:

fund availability,

reliable event-driven processing,

batching & settlement,

final debit posting,

and clear error handling (including retries & DLQ).

5. Preconditions

Customer is authenticated

The biller exists and is active

Customer account has sufficient funds

6. Postconditions
Success

Funds are debited from customer's account

Payment status is marked as POSTED

Failure

No debit occurs

Hold is released

Payment is marked FAILED

7. Trigger

Customer initiates a Bill Payment through digital channel.

8. Main Flow (Event-Driven Steps)
Step 1 – Validate Biller

Payment Orchestrator calls Biller Service:

verify biller exists

verify biller is active

Step 2 – Validate Funds

Payment Orchestrator calls Account Service:

verify balance

place temporary fund hold

Step 3 – Record Payment + Outbox (Atomic Transaction)

Insert payment row with FUNDS_HELD

Insert outbox row with PENDING

(Transactional Outbox ensures DB + Kafka consistency)

Step 4 – Ack to User

Payment Orchestrator returns HTTP 202 Accepted

Step 5 – Outbox Publisher Triggers Events

Picks pending outbox rows

Publishes events to Kafka

Marks rows as PUBLISHED

Step 6 – BillPay Worker Consumes Event

Stores each bill line

Assigns batch ID

Emits status event (BATCHED)

Step 7 – Cutoff Batching

At cutoff (e.g., 4 PM):

Collect all pending bill lines

Form a batch

Emit batch ready event

Step 8 – Settlement Processing

Settlement Service:

Builds pain.001

Uploads file to Dummy Central 1

Emits submitted event

Step 9 – Central 1 Response

When pain.002 is received:

Settlement iterates each record

Emits billpay.status for each item

Step 10 – Final Posting

Payment Orchestrator:

If POSTED → release hold + debit funds

If FAILED → release hold only

9. Error / Retry Handling

Settlement performs up to 3 retries

Persistent failures → moved to DLQ

DLQ items flagged for manual review

10. Business States

FUNDS_HELD

BATCHED

SUBMITTED

POSTED

FAILED
